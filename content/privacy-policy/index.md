ADORE.software is an initiative created by the Research Software Alliance (ReSA) and the Netherlands eScience Center, and adheres to the [eScience Center's privacy policy](https://www.esciencecenter.nl/privacy-policy/).

### How does this policy apply to ADORE.software?

We collect personal and organisational data for communication purposes only. We treat any personal data we collect with the strictest confidentiality. This privacy notice provides more detail on what information is collected, why, and what your rights are regarding these data. This notice is regularly reviewed to ensure that it is up to date and complies with legislation.

### With whom do we share your personal data?

We make use of the following service providers:

- Google (Google forms & docs)
- Mailerlite (Mailing communications)
- Sentry (Website performance and debugging)
- Motomo (for monitoring activity)
- GitHub (for coding the website)

### Cookies

This website uses a minimal amount of cookies, and only for functionality purposes.

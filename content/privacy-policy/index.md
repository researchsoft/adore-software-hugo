## How does this policy apply to ADORE.software?

ADORE.software is an initiative created by the Research Software Alliance (ReSA) and the Netherlands eScience Center, and adheres to the [eScience Center's privacy policy](https://www.esciencecenter.nl/privacy-policy/).

"We collect personal and organisational data for communication purposes only." The organization maintains strict confidentiality regarding any personal data collected. This privacy notice details what information is gathered, the reasons for collection, and user rights regarding such data. The notice undergoes regular review to maintain current compliance with applicable legislation.

## With whom do we share your personal data?

The following service providers are utilized:

- Google (Google forms & docs)
- Mailerlite (Mailing communications)
- Sentry (Website performance and debugging)
- Motomo (for monitoring activity)

## Privacy Policy

*Last updated: April 2026*

### Overview

This privacy policy describes how ADORE.software collects, uses, and protects information collected through this website.

### Information We Collect

**Signing the Declaration:** When you sign the Amsterdam Declaration, we collect:
- Name
- Organization/affiliation (optional for individuals)
- Email address
- Website or ORCID (optional)
- Country (optional)

This information is used to:
- Verify signatories
- Display names on our signatories page
- Send occasional updates about the Declaration (you can opt out)

**Newsletter Subscriptions:** If you subscribe to the ReSA newsletter, your email address is collected and managed through MailerLite in accordance with their privacy policy.

**Website Analytics:** This website may use analytics tools to understand how visitors use the site. This data is aggregated and anonymous.

### How We Use Information

We use collected information to:
- Maintain the list of Declaration signatories
- Communicate with signatories about relevant updates
- Improve the website and resources
- Understand the reach and impact of the Declaration

### Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. Information may be shared:
- Publicly (names and affiliations of signatories are displayed on this website)
- With ReSA and partner organizations for coordination purposes
- When required by law

### Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Access your personal information
- Request correction of inaccurate information
- Request removal from our signatory list
- Opt out of communications
- Request deletion of your data

To exercise these rights, please [contact us](/contact/).

### Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction.

### Third-Party Services

This website may use third-party services:
- **MailerLite** for newsletter management
- **Google Forms** for signature collection
- **Netlify** for website hosting
- **GitHub** for content management

Each service has its own privacy policy.

### Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top indicates when changes were last made.

### Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please [contact us](/contact/).

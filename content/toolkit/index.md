## The ADORE.software Toolkit

The **ADORE.software Toolkit** provides practical guidance for implementing the recommendations of the Amsterdam Declaration on Funding Research Software Sustainability.

### Purpose

The Toolkit is designed to help:

- **Funding organizations** develop and improve policies for research software
- **Research institutions** support research software and its developers
- **Researchers and RSEs** advocate for better software practices
- **Policy makers** understand the importance of research software sustainability

### Toolkit Contents

The Toolkit includes:

#### 1. Understanding Research Software

- Definitions and scope of research software
- The role of research software in modern research
- Examples across different research domains
- The software lifecycle in research contexts

#### 2. Funding Models and Practices

- Current challenges in research software funding
- Best practices from leading funders
- Different funding mechanisms and their applicability
- Long-term sustainability considerations

#### 3. Policy Frameworks

- Sample policy language for funders
- Recognition and reward mechanisms
- Career paths for research software professionals
- Training and capacity building

#### 4. Implementation Guidance

- Step-by-step implementation strategies
- Case studies from early adopters
- Resources and tools
- Metrics for success

#### 5. Community and Infrastructure

- Building sustainable communities
- Shared infrastructure approaches
- Collaboration models
- Knowledge sharing platforms

### Using the Toolkit

Organizations can use the Toolkit to:

1. **Assess** current practices and identify gaps
2. **Develop** new policies aligned with the Declaration
3. **Implement** changes systematically
4. **Evaluate** progress and impact
5. **Share** experiences with the community

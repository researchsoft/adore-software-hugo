## Amsterdam Declaration Signatories

The following individuals and organizations have signed the Amsterdam Declaration on Funding Research Software Sustainability, publicly committing to support research software and the people who develop and maintain it.

### Organizational Signatories

- **Australian Research Data Commons** - [ardc.edu.au](https://ardc.edu.au/)
- **Digital Research Alliance of Canada** - [alliancecan.ca](https://alliancecan.ca/)
- **Dutch Research Council (NWO)** - [nwo.nl](https://www.nwo.nl/en)
- **German Research Foundation (DFG)** - [dfg.de](https://www.dfg.de/)
- **Netherlands eScience Center** - [esciencecenter.nl](https://www.esciencecenter.nl/)
- **Research Council of Norway (Forskningsrådet)** - [forskningsradet.no](https://www.forskningsradet.no/en/)
- **Science for Africa Foundation** - [scienceforafrica.foundation](https://scienceforafrica.foundation/)
- **SciLifeLab** - [scilifelab.se](https://www.scilifelab.se/)
- **ZonMw** - [zonmw.nl](https://www.zonmw.nl/)

### Individual Signatories

Hundreds of individuals from around the world have signed the Declaration, including:

- Research software engineers
- Researchers and academics
- Funding agency representatives
- Research infrastructure professionals
- Policy makers and administrators

View the complete and current list on our interactive [signatories page](/signatories/).

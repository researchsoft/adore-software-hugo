## Contact Us

We welcome your questions, feedback, and ideas about the Amsterdam Declaration on Funding Research Software Sustainability.

### Get in Touch

For general inquiries about ADORE.software:

**Email:** info@researchsoft.org

### Specific Inquiries

**Signing the Declaration:** If you have questions about becoming a signatory, please see our [Get Involved](/get-involved/) page first, then email us if you need additional assistance.

**Media Inquiries:** For press and media requests, please contact info@researchsoft.org with "Media Inquiry" in the subject line.

**Partnerships and Collaborations:** We're interested in partnerships that advance research software sustainability. Please reach out to discuss potential collaborations.

### Social Media

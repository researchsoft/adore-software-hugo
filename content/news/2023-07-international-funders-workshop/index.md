The Research Software Alliance (ReSA) and the Digital Research Alliance of Canada (the Alliance) are jointly convening the International Research Software Funders Workshop from September 18-20 in Montréal, Canada, and virtually. The theme of this year’s workshop is “Investing in People: Anticipating the Future of Research Software”. During the event, funders will work together to explore how they can support sustainable research software and communities.

Find out more [here](https://adore.software/international-research-software-funders-workshop/)!

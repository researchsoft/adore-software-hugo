The next draft of the [Amsterdam Declaration on Funding Research Software Sustainability](https://click.mailerlite.com/link/c/YT0yMTc1MDgyOTgzODAxNzU1ODE1JmM9dTljNSZiPTExMDUyODU0MzAmZD1sNXczcjVv._6cwC474QNSj4hEYfrIlaZglp_A2LltzKjG9v3aMiT0) is now [open for feedback](https://forms.gle/CfmrYfSDxJguccuK9). Stakeholders who will potentially be affected by the Declaration, including anyone in the research software community, are particularly invited to [**provide responses**](https://forms.gle/CfmrYfSDxJguccuK9)**by 25 May**. Funders wishing to be involved in the consultation can also complete the [Expression of Interest](https://click.mailerlite.com/link/c/YT0yMTc1MDgyOTgzODAxNzU1ODE1JmM9dTljNSZiPTExMDUyODU0MzQmZD1iN3IwdTl2.Pl_ynJ4TOMDEOWT9F9Il4x-B-R4FBuIjXfhaXDCqZS0) in signing the Declaration, and any stakeholder can also [sign up to receive updates](https://click.mailerlite.com/link/c/YT0yMTc1MDgyOTgzODAxNzU1ODE1JmM9dTljNSZiPTExMDUyODU0MzgmZD1yOG81Zjh3.AAl-zFZeVXO3G4O0d9hJPhDi1UCkIcivyZqRhzgWTLg) on the progress of the Declaration.

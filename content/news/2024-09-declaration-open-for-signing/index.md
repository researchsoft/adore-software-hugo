The Amsterdam Declaration on Funding Research Software Sustainability (ADORE.software) has now broadened the criteria of who can be a signatory.

**The Declaration can be signed by individuals and organisations that support research software and/or the people who develop and maintain it.**

By becoming a Signatory, individuals and organisations publicly state that they agree with the principles and recommendations outlined in the Amsterdam Declaration.

<!--more-->

## Who Can Sign?

The Declaration is now open to:

- **Individuals**: Researchers, research software engineers, developers, and anyone who supports research software
- **Organizations**: Funding bodies, research institutions, universities, companies, and professional associations
- **Communities**: Research software communities and initiatives

## Why Sign?

Signing the Declaration shows your commitment to:

- Recognizing research software as fundamental research infrastructure
- Supporting the people who develop and maintain research software
- Improving funding practices for long-term software sustainability
- Promoting open, FAIR, and sustainable research software

## How to Sign

Visit our [Get Involved](/get-involved/) page to sign the Declaration as an individual or organization.

Your signature will add to the growing international movement to improve research software sustainability!

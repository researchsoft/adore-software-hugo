SciLifeLab will co-organize an international research software funders workshop as part of Open Science efforts in Sweden and beyond.

<!--more-->

## SciLifeLab's Role

SciLifeLab, Sweden's national infrastructure for life science research, is collaborating with the Research Software Alliance (ReSA) and other partners to organize an international workshop for research funders.

The workshop will focus on:

- Best practices for funding research software
- Implementation of the Amsterdam Declaration recommendations
- International coordination among funders
- Supporting research software communities

## Why This Matters

Research software is essential for modern science, yet funding models often don't adequately support long-term sustainability. This workshop brings together funders to address these challenges collectively.

## SciLifeLab's Commitment

By co-organizing this workshop, SciLifeLab demonstrates its commitment to:

- Advancing Open Science practices
- Supporting research software sustainability
- Leading international collaboration
- Implementing evidence-based funding policies

SciLifeLab is also a signatory of the Amsterdam Declaration, aligning their practices with international best standards for research software funding.

## About the Amsterdam Declaration

The Amsterdam Declaration provides practical recommendations for funders to improve research software sustainability. Learn more at [adore.software](/declaration/).

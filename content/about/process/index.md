## Process

### About the process

The adoption process for the Declaration, as discussed during the International Funders Workshop: The Future of Research Software includes a series of opportunities for community input to gather feedback and refine the Declaration and adoption process.

### Timeline

The following is the proposed timeline for the adoption of the Declaration:

**November 2022**

International Funders Workshop: The Future of Research Software

**November 2022 – March 2023**

Drafting next version of declaration.

**March 2023 – August 2023**

Collecting & integrating feedback from potential signatories and other stakeholders.

**September 2023**

Signature of final version of the declaration. This will be the initial signing event; organisations may sign at a later stage.

**Late 2024 (TBC)**

One year after signing, signatories share their initial steps in implementing the Declaration in their own organisation.

**Sometime 2029 (TBC)**

Signatories demonstrate progress on a regular basis, with a contact moment five years after signing.

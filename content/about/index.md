## About the Amsterdam Declaration

The **Amsterdam Declaration on Funding Research Software Sustainability** (ADORE.software) is an international initiative to improve funding practices for research software.

### Mission

Our mission is to:

- Raise awareness of the critical role research software plays in research outcomes
- Improve funding practices to support research software sustainability
- Build consensus among funders on best practices for research software
- Provide practical guidance through the ADORE.software Toolkit

### Background

Research software has become increasingly critical to research across all disciplines. Studies show that:

- More than 90% of researchers acknowledge software as important to their research
- About 70% of researchers say their research would not be possible without software
- Research papers in top journals mention an average of 6.5 distinct pieces of software

Despite this importance, research software often lacks adequate support for long-term sustainability.

### The Declaration Process

The Amsterdam Declaration was developed through:

1. Extensive consultation with international stakeholders
2. Workshops with research funders and policy makers
3. Input from research software engineers and developers
4. Review by the research software community

### Get Involved

You can support the Amsterdam Declaration by:

- [Signing the Declaration](/get-involved/) as an individual or organization
- Using the [Toolkit](/toolkit/) to improve your organization's practices
- Sharing information about ADORE.software with your network
- Attending [events and workshops](https://www.researchsoft.org/events/)

### Related Initiatives

ADORE.software is supported by the [Research Software Alliance (ReSA)](https://www.researchsoft.org/), which brings together research software communities to collaborate on the advancement of research software.

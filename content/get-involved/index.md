## Join the Movement

The Amsterdam Declaration is open for all to sign! You can become a signatory to publicly show your support for improving research software sustainability.

### Who Can Sign?

The Declaration can be signed by:

- **Individuals** who support research software and the people who develop and maintain it
- **Organizations** including funding bodies, research institutions, universities, and companies
- **Research software communities** and professional associations

By becoming a signatory, you publicly state that you agree with the principles and recommendations of the Amsterdam Declaration.

### How to Sign

#### For Individuals

To sign as an individual:

1. Review the [Amsterdam Declaration](/declaration/)
2. Fill out the [signing form](https://forms.gle/example) with your details:
   - Name
   - Affiliation (optional)
   - Website or ORCID (optional)
3. Submit the form

Your name will be added to the [list of signatories](/signatories/) after verification.

#### For Organizations

To sign as an organization:

1. Review the [Amsterdam Declaration](/declaration/)
2. Ensure organizational approval to become a signatory
3. Fill out the [organizational signing form](https://forms.gle/example) with:
   - Organization name
   - Representative name and title
   - Organization website
   - Logo (optional, for website display)
4. Submit the form

Your organization will be added to the [list of signatories](/signatories/) after verification.

### Other Ways to Get Involved

Beyond signing, you can support ADORE.software by:

#### Share and Advocate

- Share information about the Declaration with your networks
- Use **#ADOREsoftware** on social media
- Present about ADORE.software at conferences and meetings
- Reference the Declaration in policy documents

#### Implement the Recommendations

- Use the [ADORE.software Toolkit](/toolkit/) to improve practices
- Develop policies aligned with the Declaration
- Share your implementation experiences
- Provide case studies for the Toolkit

#### Participate in Events

- Attend ADORE.software workshops and webinars
- Join Research Software Alliance [events](https://www.researchsoft.org/events/)
- Organize local discussions about research software sustainability
- Connect with other signatories and advocates

#### Stay Informed

- Subscribe to the [ReSA newsletter](https://dashboard.mailerlite.com/forms/778129/110635094443558050/share)
- Follow [#ADOREsoftware on Twitter](https://twitter.com/search?q=%23AdoreSoftware)
- Join the Research Software Alliance [Slack community](https://researchsoft.slack.com/)
- Check this website regularly for updates
